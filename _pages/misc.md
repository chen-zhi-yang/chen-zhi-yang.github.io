#### Competitive Programming

I *was* a contestant of algorithmic competitive programming, and won silver medals in [National Olympiad in Informatics (NOI)](https://www.noi.cn) in high school. I always feel a sweet and melancholy nostalgia for NOI. It led me to the world of computer science, yet it also utterly transformed my heart and life. Nevertheless, those never-coming-back days spent discussing algorithmic problems with high school friends remain my most treasured memories, regardless of everything else.

#### Literature

I am a humble reader of modern literature and literary theory. A long (but not exhaustive) list of authors that I like: Roberto Bolaño, Julio Cortázar, William Faulkner, James Joyce, Richard Yates, Franz Kafka, Anton Chekhov, Fyodor Dostoevsky, Mishima Yukio, Walter Benjamin, and Roland Barthes. [Bolaño](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roberto_Bola%C3%B1o) is my personal favorite (though this does not mean he is the best). When I read *Last Evenings on Earth* for the first time, I realized that he is an author I will never be able to live without.
